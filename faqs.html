Frequently Asked Questions

Bookings & Reservations

How do I book a slot at Game Veda?
You can book online via our Book Now page. Fill in your details and we'll send you a confirmation via WhatsApp. You can also call or WhatsApp us directly at +91 97176 89596.
Is advance booking required?
We recommend booking at least 1 day in advance, especially on weekends. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability.
Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?
Yes! Please contact us at least 4 hours before your slot to cancel or reschedule. Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 97176 89596.
Can I book for a group or birthday party?
Absolutely! We love group bookings. Please call or WhatsApp us for group rates and special party packages.

Pricing & Payments

What are the VR gaming prices?
VR Experience: ₹229 for 45 Min, ₹499 for 90 Min, and ₹949 for 135 Min. We also have great combo deals starting at ₹299!
What are the PlayStation pricing options?
PlayStation pricing: ₹160 for 1 Hour, ₹280 for 2 Hours (Most Popular), and ₹500 for 4 Hours (Best Value).
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and all major debit/credit cards.
Are prices inclusive of taxes?
Yes, all displayed prices are inclusive of applicable taxes.

Membership Plans

What membership plans do you offer?
We offer three plans — Silver (₹1,499 / 60h / 30 days), Gold (₹2,599 / 120h / 30 days), and Platinum (₹3,299 / 180h / 30 days). See our Membership section for full details.
Can membership hours be carried forward?
No, membership hours expire at the end of the validity period (30 days). We recommend choosing a plan that matches your gaming frequency.
Can I use my membership for VR as well?
Memberships provide access to all zones. However, VR sessions are charged separately due to equipment costs. Members get a discount on VR sessions.
