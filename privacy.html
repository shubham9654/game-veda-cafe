Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Introduction

Game Veda ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected when you use our website, make a booking, or visit our premises.

By using our services, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. Last updated: May 2025.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification: Name, phone number (WhatsApp), when you make a booking or contact us.
  • Booking Details: Date, time, and package selected for your session.
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics about which pages you visit on our website (no personally identifiable data).
  • Communications: Any messages you send us via WhatsApp, email, or our contact form.

We do not collect sensitive financial data like card numbers. All payments are processed at the venue through secure terminals.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and manage your gaming slot bookings.
  • To send you booking reminders and confirmations via WhatsApp.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • To send occasional promotional offers (only if you have opted in).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third party. We may share your data only in the following limited cases:

  • With trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website or business (e.g., WhatsApp for communications), who are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process (e.g., court order).
  • To protect the safety and rights of our staff, customers, or the public.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Booking records are typically retained for up to 12 months.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses browser Local Storage to remember your theme preference (dark/light mode). This data is stored locally on your device and is never sent to our servers.

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies on our website.
